=== GoodNotes Desktop App ===

RUN:
  Double-click launch_electron.bat
  OR: npm start

  Documents\GoodNotes_App_Data\notebooks\

EXPORTS (PDF/PNG):
  Documents\GoodNotes_App_Data\exports\

SETTINGS:
  Documents\GoodNotes_App_Data\settings.json
